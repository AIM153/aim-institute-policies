AIM Institute App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12th October 2026
Last updated: 12th October 2026

1. Introduction

AIM Institute provides the AIM Institute App to support educational and administrative activities. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when students, teachers, staff and other authorised users use the App.

The Institute should handle personal information in accordance with applicable law and this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the user's role and the features used, the App may process the following information:

Account and contact information

Name and email address.

Phone number, where provided.

School, college or other institute-related information.

Account approval status and user role.

Academic and institute information

Assigned batches, courses and enrolment information.

Access permissions and relevant student or staff records.

Academic materials, notices and course-related information made available through the App.

Fee and administrative records

Fee amounts, balances and payment status.

Payment methods, receipt references and related administrative records, where maintained by the Institute.

Technical information

Device notification tokens used to deliver push notifications.

Other technical information that may be processed by the App's service providers, depending on the services and configurations used.

Communications

Information users choose to include when contacting the Institute for support or making a privacy request.

The exact information processed must be verified against the App's current implementation and service-provider settings before this policy is published.

3. How Information Is Used

The Institute may use relevant information to:

Create, verify and manage user accounts.

Approve registrations and administer access permissions.

Assign batches and courses.

Provide academic materials, notices and notifications.

Maintain and correct relevant fee and administrative records.

Respond to support requests and privacy requests.

Protect the App, investigate suspected misuse and maintain security.

Meet applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.

Information should only be used for purposes permitted by applicable law.

4. Services and Information Sharing

The App uses or may rely on the following services for its operation:

Firebase Authentication: user authentication and account access.

Cloud Firestore: storage of App-related records.

Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivery of push notifications.

Google Drive: access to study materials through external links, where provided.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to the applicable service terms, configurations and privacy documentation.

Authorised Institute personnel may access information when necessary for legitimate educational, administrative, support or security purposes.

The Institute should not disclose personal information to unrelated parties unless there is an appropriate permitted basis, such as the user's valid direction or consent where required, a necessary service-provider arrangement, or a legal obligation.

The Institute must verify the actual data flows and applicable provider terms before publication.

5. Notifications

The App may use device notification tokens to send institute notices, academic updates or other relevant administrative communications.

Users may be able to disable notifications through their device settings. Disabling notifications may prevent them from receiving updates promptly.

6. Third-Party Links

The App may link to external services, including Google Drive. When users open an external service, its own privacy policy and terms may apply.

The Institute does not control every aspect of external services and encourages users to review their applicable terms and privacy information.

7. Data Retention

The Institute will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Certain fee, accounting or administrative records may need to be retained after an account is deleted where a legal or legitimate recordkeeping obligation applies.

Where information is no longer required, the Institute should delete it or otherwise handle it appropriately, subject to applicable requirements.

Specific retention periods and the treatment of records retained after account deletion must be confirmed by the Institute before this policy is finalised.

8. Account Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request account deletion by following the instructions on the  https://aim153.github.io/aim-institute-policies/ . The Institute will verify and process requests in accordance with its procedures and applicable requirements. In-app account-deletion functionality is being prepared and will be made available before the App is released through Google Play.
9. Security

The Institute will take reasonable measures appropriate to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their credentials and notify the Institute if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

10. Younger Users

The App may be used by people of different ages. The Institute will apply the consent requirements and safeguards required by applicable law when processing information relating to younger users.

The Institute's lack of a general minimum-age restriction does not remove any legal obligations relating to children or parental or guardian involvement.

11. User Requests and Concerns

For privacy questions, corrections to personal information or account-deletion requests, contact the Institute using the details in Section 13.

The Institute will review requests and respond in accordance with applicable requirements. Some requests may require identity verification to protect users' information.

12. Changes to This Policy

The Institute may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its data practices or applicable requirements change.

The updated policy will display its revised date and be made available through the App or the Institute's policy website. Where required, the Institute will provide notice and obtain any necessary consent.
