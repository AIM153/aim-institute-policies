# AIM Institute App — Account Deletion Request

## Request deletion of your account

Students and other users of the AIM Institute App may request deletion of their app account and associated personal information using the process below.

### How to request deletion

Send an email to **aimhelpforstudents@gmail.com** with the subject:

**AIM Institute App — Account Deletion Request**

Please include the email address registered with your AIM Institute App account. Do not send your password or other login credentials.

The Institute may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request.

### What happens next?

After verifying the request, AIM Institute will review and process the account-deletion request within a reasonable period and inform you of the outcome.

The Institute will delete associated personal information that it is not required or otherwise permitted to retain. Certain fee, payment or accounting records may be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Any retained information will be handled in accordance with the Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

Deleting your app account may remove your access to app features. It does not automatically cancel admission, course enrolment or outstanding fee obligations.

### Privacy Policy

Read the [AIM Institute App Privacy Policy](privacy) to learn more about information handling and retention.
